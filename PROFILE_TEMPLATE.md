## 🖼️ Profile Avatar

**Requirements on Codester:** Square image, minimum 200×200 px

**Tips:**
- Use a professional headshot (real photo builds the most trust)
- Alternatively, use a clean logo/avatar with your initials
- Avoid cartoon avatars on a professional marketplace
- Good background: solid color (deep blue, dark gray, or white)

**Suggested AI avatar if needed:** Generate a professional headshot-style image with a clean background using tools like `thispersondoesnotexist.com` or Midjourney.
